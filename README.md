# ScholarshipConnect BD

ScholarshipConnect BD is a web-based platform designed to help students discover scholarships, check their eligibility, connect with other students and scholarship recipients, and manage scholarship applications from one place.

## Project Overview

Finding suitable scholarships can be difficult because scholarship information is often scattered across university websites, government portals, social media groups, and other sources.

ScholarshipConnect BD aims to provide a centralized platform where students can find relevant scholarship opportunities, receive deadline notifications, get guidance, and manage their scholarship applications efficiently.

## Objectives

* Centralize scholarship information in one platform
* Help students find scholarships based on their eligibility
* Provide scholarship deadline notifications
* Allow students to manage their scholarship applications
* Connect students with scholarship recipients and mentors
* Make scholarship searching easier and more organized

## Key Features

* Scholarship Search
* Scholarship Details and Eligibility Information
* Eligibility-Based Scholarship Recommendations
* Scholarship Deadline Tracking
* Application Management
* Student Networking
* Mentor and Scholarship Recipient Guidance
* Notifications and Updates
* Student Profile Management
